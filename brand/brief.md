# Brief (working name: Logowright)

- **Name:** Logowright (working name until Matt picks; alternates Stampwright, Brandry, brand-kit). Lower case likely: `logowright`.
- **What it does:** A Claude Code plugin that draws a logo as clean SVG from a short brief, lets you pick the type by eye, and exports the whole brand kit around it from one `brand.json`.
- **Who it's for:** Indie makers and small teams with a side project, app or shop who want a real logo and a kit without hiring a studio, and designers who want the boring exports done.
- **Adjectives:** crafted, precise, friendly, a little nerdy.
- **Colours:** none to keep (it has no identity yet; Harbor's navy and orange belong to the example brand, so avoid reusing them).
- **Category clichés:** the pen nib, the pencil, the paintbrush, the magic wand and sparkles, the colour wheel, the Bézier pen tool with handles, the generic "AI" star, a robot.
- **Category audit:** AI logo makers (Looka, Tailor Brands, Brandmark, Hatchful, Canva's) lean on purple-to-pink gradients, sparkles and rounded sans wordmarks; brand-management tools (Frontify, Brandfolder) use quiet geometric sans and blue. Room to stand apart: one flat ink colour plus a warm accent, a drawn, engineered mark, and the idea of craft (a wright) over magic.

## Concepts (fifteen, before the round)

Symbols
1. A maker's mark: a round stamp whose impression is itself a tiny logo (the tool that makes marks, leaving a mark).
2. A wax seal with a grid pressed into it (the 256 grid as the seal's face).
3. A compass (drafting) drawing a perfect circle: construction, not magic.
4. A single circle and a square overlapping, with the lens between them in the accent (geometry is the material).
5. A letterpress block, the reversed letter visible on the block's face.
6. A favicon tile sitting on a larger app icon tile, sizes in a stack (the kit, small to large).

Lettermarks / monograms
7. An L drawn with its construction circle left in (the corner fillet as a visible arc).
8. A w made of two V-shaped drafting set squares.
9. An L and W as a shared stem in a square tile (monogram, since Logo + wright).
10. A lowercase l whose foot is a ruler with tick marks.

Wordmarks
11. logowright in a crisp geometric sans with the o's drawn as circles with a centre point (compass prick).
12. logowright with the g's lower bowl as a construction circle in the accent.
13. The word set twice, once as outline paths (with nodes) and once filled: the word and its own working.

Odd / weird
14. A wright's mallet whose head is a favicon tile.
15. A logo eating its own export: the mark at 512 shrinking along a size ramp to a 16 px dot (the size ramp as the logo).
16. A rubber stamp that stamps a brand on itself.

## Round 1 grid (if Logowright is picked)

Rows: symbol, lettermark/monogram, wordmark. Columns: weirdness 2, 3, 4.
- Symbol: 4 (circle and square lens) · 1 (maker's stamp) · 15 (size ramp)
- Lettermark: 7 (L with its construction arc) · 9 (LW shared stem tile) · 8 (w from set squares)
- Wordmark: 11 (compass-point o's) · 12 (g on a construction circle) · 13 (outline and filled)

Type shortlist to test: geometric (Outfit, Manrope, Space Grotesk is Harbor's, so not that), grotesque (Inter Tight, Schibsted Grotesk), mono for weirdness 4 (JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono).

Specimen (`rounds/01/specimen.png`): front-runners Outfit 600 (#2, true-circle o's for the compass-point idea), Jost 500 (#18, Futura-like, drafting feel) and Red Hat Display 600 (#16, friendlier). Avoid Fredoka (Blobscape's) and Space Grotesk (Harbor's).
